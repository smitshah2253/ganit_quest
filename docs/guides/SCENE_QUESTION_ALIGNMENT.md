# Educational Alignment Audit (Question-by-Question)

**Audit Criteria (Educational POV):**
A scene is considered **✅ Aligned** if its gameplay and UI properly convey the underlying mathematical concepts such that a standard 10th-grade student can easily learn from it and prepare for their board exams. Abstract geometric representations (like a simple right triangle for a "helicopter" problem, or plotted points for a "drone") are perfectly acceptable and highly educational because they teach the student how to extract the mathematical model from a real-world word problem.

A scene is considered **🔴 Unaligned** ONLY if the mathematical structure or interactive geometry is misleading, missing, or fails to represent the specific board exam concept (e.g., the question requires two overlapping triangles, but the scene only draws one).
